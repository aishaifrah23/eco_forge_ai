Section 04 · Architecture Details

        
The 4-Agent Orchestration Ecosystem

        

          Coordinated through the IBM Bob agent framework to ensure that safety screening strictly precedes any lab dispatch.
        

      


      

        
        

          

            

              01
            

            
Component Triage Agent

            Model: Granite 3.0-8B-Instruct
            

              Processes silkscreen OCR, FCC IDs, and part numbers to extract a hierarchical Reverse-BOM. Classifies chip packages (QFN, SOIC, DIP) and salvage viability.
            

          

          

            Output: List<HarvestedComponent>
          

        


        

          

            

              02
            

            
Chemical Safety Auditor

            Grounding: CPCB 2022 & IEEE 1874
            

              Acts as a regulatory gatekeeper. Scans for pre-2006 leaded solder (Sn60Pb40) and triggers Class-D dry sand quarantine for swollen lithium batteries.
            

          

          

            Output: SafetyAuditResult
          

        


        

          

            

              03
            

            
Campus Inventory Matcher

            Engine: ChromaDB Vector Search
            

              Matches harvested components against student project requests across robotics societies, IoT research cells, and electric vehicle clubs at VIT Bhopal.
            

          

          

            Output: RequisitionMatch
          

        


        

          

            

              04
            

            
Circular Credit Dispatcher

            Metric: Avoided Embodied Carbon
            

              Computes virgin silicon displacement emissions (~1.42 kg CO₂e per IC) and mints tokenized circular credits to depositing hostels or departments.
            

          

          

            Output: CircularCreditDispatch
          

        


      


    

  


  
  

    

      
      

        
Section 05 · Regulatory Grounding

        
CPCB E-Waste Rules 2022 & IEEE 1874 RAG

        

          Statutory standards indexed inside the ChromaDB vector database:
        

      


      

        

          
CPCB Schedule II (RoHS Norms)

          
Heavy Metal Concentration Ceilings

          

            Lead (Pb) < 0.1%, Mercury (Hg) < 0.1%, Cadmium (Cd) < 0.01%, Hexavalent Chromium < 0.1%. Open-flame desoldering is strictly illegal under Indian law.
          

        


        

          
IEEE Standard 1874-2018

          
Component Reusability Screening

          

            Screening protocols for surface mount ICs: pad oxidation index < 5%, intermetallic bonding intactness, and operating thermal delta < 15°C under nominal load.
          

        


        

          
IS 13252 / Battery Rules 2022

          
Lithium Fire Mitigation

          

            Immediate quarantine protocol for lithium pouch cells showing visible gas swelling, puncture, or voltage below 2.5V/cell. Mandates dry sand containment bins.
